**Machine Readable page post (draft):**

A third of Nashville's police incident ledger is not crime.

We pulled all 945,811 records in the Metro Nashville Police Department's incident layer, 2019 to November 3, 2026, and split them the way the department's own metadata organizes its codes. 34% sit under MNPD's non-criminal incident types: police inquiries, found and lost property, transports. The single biggest code in the file is "matter of record."

Underneath that mix, the crime side moved. Records under criminal offense codes fell 9.9% in Jan-Oct 2025 and another 10.1% in Jan-Oct 2026. The drop is concentrated: vehicle break-ins and thefts down by nearly half against 2023, robbery down 71% against 2019. Simple assault did not fall at all.

And the ledger's most quotable statistic is an artifact: 31.7% of records read UNFOUNDED, but 96% of those sit under the non-criminal codes. Inside criminal offenses, the unfounded share is 1.8%.

This is the second locality where we have run this decomposition - Denver's ledger showed the same structure in October. Two cities, two record systems, one lesson: never quote a police ledger's total until you have split it by what the codes actually are.
